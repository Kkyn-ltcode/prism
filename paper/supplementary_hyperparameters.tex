\quad GNN Layers ($T$) & $2$ & Message-passing iterations \\

\quad Output Head & Linear($d_{\mathrm{hidden}}$, 1) & Single scalar prediction \\

\quad SIREN Init ($\mathbf{W}_2$) & $\mathcal{U}(-\sqrt{6/d_h}/\omega_0, \sqrt{6/d_h}/\omega_0)$ & Hidden layer ($d_h = 4$; Sitzmann et al.~\cite{sitzmann2020siren}) \\

\quad PQC Layers ($L$) & $2$ & Default; varied in circuit scaling study \\
\quad Differentiation & Backpropagation & Via PennyLane autograd interface \\

\quad Training Time (Q-PRISM) & $\sim 3$\,min / seed & $N = 1{,}000$, $15$ epochs (PennyLane statevector simulation) \\
